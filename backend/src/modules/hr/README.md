# HR module boundary

HR owns employment/profile and professional-evidence behavior in the approved
HR scope. HR does not own system accounts, roles, permissions, sessions, or
clinical-record access grants.

When another module needs practitioner facts, HR will publish them through an
approved typed in-process port. A job title or employee status alone must never
be interpreted as a clinical authorization grant.

V.1 contains no HR business behavior, schema, routes, or account provisioning.
